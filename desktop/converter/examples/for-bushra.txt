A Note Before You Begin

This little reader was built for you, Bushra.

Every part of it was chosen with you in mind. The screen holds its page

without power, the way a real book does, so it can sit on the table

beside you and simply wait until you pick it up again. No glare, no

notifications, nothing asking for your attention. Just the page you left

open.

It remembers where you stopped reading. When you come back, it opens

exactly there.

How It Works

Turn the wheel to move through a page. Press it to choose something.

MENU opens the menu. EXIT goes back.

When you leave it alone it goes to sleep on its own, and the page stays

on the glass. Touch anything and it wakes.

That is the whole thing. It was made to get out of your way.

Why I Made It

Because you deserve something that was made rather than bought.

Everything else will be added over time: the Quran with its

translations, the books you want to keep close, the memorisation tools.

That work is still ahead. But this page had to be first, because a thing

built for someone should say who it is for.

It is for you.

With all my love, Fahad
